# Changelog

## 0.2.0
- Reverse search: Ctrl/Cmd+click in a LaTeX PDF jumps to the matching line (and word) of the `.tex` source, using SyncTeX.

## 0.1.1
- The sidebar starts hidden, even for PDFs that ask to open with their outline or thumbnails.

## 0.1.0
- First version: view and annotate PDFs (highlight, comments, free text, drawing, images, signatures), saved into the PDF.
- Backs up annotations when the PDF is overwritten by another program and offers to re-apply them to the new version.
